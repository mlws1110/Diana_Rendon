Professional Growth Reflection

Danielson Framework Component 3B

Initial Assessment - May 2024

During my formal observation on teaching main ideas, I received a "Developing" rating in Danielson Framework Component 3B: Using Questioning and Discussion Techniques. At that time, my questioning strategies primarily focused on recall-based questions, with limited opportunities for higher-order thinking and student-led discussions.

Implementation of Improvements

Following this feedback, I implemented intentional strategies to enhance student engagement through questioning:

  • Incorporated open-ended questions and wait time
  • Integrated student-to-student discourse techniques
  • Implemented structured sentence stems
  • Facilitated small-group discussions

Growth Outcome - June 2024

By June 2024, during my informal observation, I demonstrated significant growth in this area:

  • Students actively engaged in discussions
  • Students responded to each other's ideas
  • Students justified answers using textual evidence
  • Achieved "Effective" rating in Component 3B
