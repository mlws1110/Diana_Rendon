School Leadership

Leadership Initiatives at PS 105

I have actively contributed to the leadership and growth of PS 105 by engaging in various initiatives that support student achievement, school culture, and teacher collaboration.

Academic Support & Intervention (2022)

I worked in the compensatory services program after school using the Spiral program, providing targeted academic interventions to students who required additional support. Through individualized instruction, I helped students strengthen foundational skills, improve comprehension, and build confidence in their learning.

School Event Support (2024)

I played a role in fostering a positive school culture by assisting with the school fashion show, ensuring a well-organized and engaging experience for students. My involvement helped create an inclusive and community-driven atmosphere.

School Committee & Spirit Initiatives (2025)

As a member of the school committee focused on school spirit, I have contributed ideas and initiatives to enhance student engagement and pride in our school. Through planned events and activities, I have worked to strengthen school morale and create a sense of belonging among students and staff.

Teacher Collaboration & Professional Growth

I work closely with my teaching team to share and implement differentiated instructional strategies that support diverse learners. By exchanging best practices and discussing student progress, I contribute to a culture of continuous learning and improvement among educators.

Student Enrichment (4th Grade)

I have provided enrichment opportunities for fourth-grade students, focusing on extending their learning beyond the curriculum. By incorporating engaging activities and higher-level thinking tasks, I support students in developing critical thinking, problem-solving, and creativity.

Through these leadership roles, I have demonstrated my commitment to student success, school improvement, and professional collaboration, reinforcing my dedication to the mission and values of PS 105.
