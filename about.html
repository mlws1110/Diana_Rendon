Professional Biography

Dedicated special education teacher with a strong academic background and diverse experience in inclusive education. Earned a Bachelor's degree from Berkeley College and a Master's degree in Special Education with Bilingual Extension from City College. Started as a paraprofessional for three years before completing the Teacher Fellows program. Passionate about creating engaging learning environments that support diverse learners and promote inclusive education.

Teaching Philosophy

In my classroom, I strive to create an inclusive environment where all students feel valued and capable of success. I believe that:

  • Every child has unique strengths and learning styles that should be celebrated
  • Effective special education requires collaboration between teachers, families, and support staff
  • Students with special needs benefit from both specialized instruction and inclusion
  • Building positive relationships is the foundation of successful teaching
  • Data-driven instruction helps identify student needs and track progress
