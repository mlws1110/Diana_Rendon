Professional Journey

As a dedicated educator, my journey has been marked by continuous growth and professional development. I was honored to be selected as a teacher fellow, which led me to achieve my bilingual license for grades 1-6 during the summer of 2021-2022. During this time, I had the valuable opportunity to mentor and teach ENL to 6th-grade students at a school in Queens, while also participating in comprehensive skill-building sessions.

Working at P.S. 105 The Bay School has been an incredible journey of professional growth. Each day brings new opportunities to learn and develop alongside my dedicated colleagues and grade team. The supportive environment has enhanced my confidence in content delivery and teaching practices, allowing me to provide the best possible education for my students.

This portfolio presents evidence of my qualifications for tenure, demonstrating excellence across three essential areas of practice in the New York City Department of Education framework: Student Learning, Teacher Practice, and Professional Contributions.
